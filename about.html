Cooking for campus, one plate at a time.

We started Five Loaves & Two Fish because we believed students deserved real, home-style Nigerian meals — affordable, fresh, and made with care.

Our story

What started as a small food service at Queen Idia Hall has grown into one of the most-loved spots on campus. We cook everything fresh every day — no shortcuts, no reheats — because that's the only way to do it right.

Our name comes from the biblical miracle of five loaves and two fish feeding a multitude. That spirit of generosity — giving everyone enough, with quality they'll remember — is what we bring to every meal we serve.

"Good food shouldn't be a luxury for students. It should be a daily given."

Every dish on our menu was chosen because it belongs at a Nigerian table. We keep the variety wide so whether you want jollof rice, amala, or just a quick side, you find something you love.

What we stand for

Three principles we never compromise on, no matter how busy service gets.

Freshness, every day

We cook fresh each morning. If it wasn't made today, it doesn't go on your plate. Every scoop is cooked to order as much as possible.

Honest pricing

Campus budgets are real budgets. Our prices reflect that — generous portions at rates a student can actually afford, without cutting corners on taste.

Home-style quality

We cook the way you'd cook at home — real ingredients, real seasoning, real care. No industrial shortcuts. Just the food your mother would approve.
