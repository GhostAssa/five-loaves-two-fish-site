How it works

Simple, fast, and straight to your door on campus.

  1. 1
    Browse the menu

    Check out our full menu — packages, dishes, soups, proteins, and add-ons. Add items to your cart as you go.

  2. 2
    Open your cart

    Review what you've picked. Enter your name, phone number, and delivery location on campus.

  3. 3
    Send via WhatsApp

    Tap "Send Order to WhatsApp" — your order goes straight to us. We'll confirm and get cooking right away.

  4. 4
    Receive your food

    We deliver anywhere on UI campus for ₦300 (plus ₦200 for the pack). Expect your food within 20–35 minutes.

  5. 5
    Enjoy your meal

    Every dish is cooked fresh to order. Leave us a review if you loved it — it means the world to us.
